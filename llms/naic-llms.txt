## Top-Level Navigation
  - [Regulator](https://content.naic.org/regulator): We provide tools and resources to help regulators set standards and best practices, provide regulatory support functions, and educate on U.S. state-based insurance regulation.
  - [Industry](https://content.naic.org/industry): Access forms, tools, and resources for individuals and organizations to file forms, improve efficiency, and remain compliant.
  - [Consumer](https://content.naic.org/consumer): Discover tools and resources to help you understand different types of insurance, claims processes, and practical tips to help support you through every stage of your life. 
  - [Committees](https://content.naic.org/committees): View actions considered by committees, task forces, and subgroups, as well as charges, schedules, and staff links.
  - [Resource Center](https://content.naic.org/resource-center): Explore recent publications, the latest news articles, new federal and legislative news, and model laws to help inform your approach.
  - [Insurance Departments](https://content.naic.org/state-insurance-departments): Find contact information for insurance departments and local insurance agents, or file a complaint.
  - [About](https://content.naic.org/about): The NAIC provides expertise, data, and analysis for insurance commissioners to effectively regulate the insurance industry and protect consumers.
